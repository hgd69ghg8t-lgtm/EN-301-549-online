This is an unofficial HTML version of the {{SOURCE_MONTH_YEAR}} final draft of EN 301 549, the European accessibility standard for information and communication technology (ICT) products and services.

This HTML edition is designed to make the standard easier to navigate, easier to read with assistive technology, and easier to link to directly — for example, to a single numbered requirement.

The draft is still under approval and may change. The official ETSI PDF remains the source of record.

Status last checked: {{STATUS_LAST_CHECKED}}.

Not an official publication. This site is an unofficial re-typesetting of the ETSI deliverable for reading convenience. ETSI, CEN and CENELEC have not endorsed it. See About this HTML edition and the accessibility statement.
