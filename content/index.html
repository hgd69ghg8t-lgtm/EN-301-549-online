EN 301 549 Online

Accessible HTML edition of ETSI EN 301 549 V4.1.0 (2026-06), Final draft: Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services.

This site republishes the standard as a WCAG 2.2 AA-conformant website: every clause and annex is its own page, with a persistent contents menu, an on-page table of contents, and next/previous navigation between clauses — so the requirements can be read, linked to and cited directly, without a PDF reader.

Not an official publication. This is an unofficial accessible re-typesetting of the ETSI deliverable for reading convenience. The ETSI PDF remains the authoritative version; see About this standard.
