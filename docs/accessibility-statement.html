This statement covers the accessibility of this website (the HTML edition of ETSI EN 301 549), not the accessibility of any product or service being evaluated against the standard it reproduces.

Target standard

This site is designed and tested with the aim of meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That is a target, not a certified or independently audited conformance claim — see Testing status below for exactly what has and has not been checked.

Testing status

The following automated checks have been run against every generated page in this site:

  • An automated accessibility scan (axe-core) against the WCAG 2.0 A/AA, 2.1 AA and 2.2 AA rule sets.
  • Structural HTML validation: heading hierarchy, a single non-empty <h1> per page, no duplicate IDs, no malformed/unclosed tags.
  • Internal link and anchor-target validation (no broken same-site links, no dangling # fragments).

Automated tools can only catch a subset of accessibility problems. They cannot judge whether alt text is actually meaningful, whether reading order makes sense to a real screen reader user, whether keyboard interaction feels usable rather than merely operable, or how the site behaves with specific assistive technology. A full manual evaluation, including real screen-reader and keyboard-only testing, has not yet been completed. See the manual testing checklist for the maintained record of what has actually been tested, by whom, and when.

Known limitations

  • Manual screen-reader testing across the combinations listed below has not yet been recorded as complete — treat any specific assistive-technology compatibility claim as untested until the log in the manual testing checklist says otherwise.
  • Some tables in the standard (particularly in Annex C and Annex ZA/ZB) are wide and require horizontal scrolling on narrow viewports. These are marked as scrollable, focusable regions with an accessible label, but very complex tables can still be harder to follow with a screen reader than the surrounding prose.
  • This is a large, mostly text-based reference document. No attempt has been made to simplify or restructure the underlying legal/technical language of the standard itself for plain-language readability — that would change the meaning of a normative document and is out of scope for this project.

Please include the page URL, what you were trying to do, the assistive technology and browser you were using if relevant, and what happened instead of what you expected.

Browsers and assistive technology

The combinations below are the ones this project intends to test against. Do not treat any of them as verified until they are recorded as tested in the manual testing log.

  • NVDA with Firefox or Chrome (Windows)
  • JAWS with Chrome or Edge (Windows)
  • VoiceOver with Safari (macOS)
  • VoiceOver (iOS)
  • TalkBack (Android)
