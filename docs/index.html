Accessible HTML edition of ‘ETSI EN 301 549 V4.1.0 (2026-06), Final draft: Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services’.

This site republishes the standard as a website designed and tested with the aim of meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA: every clause and annex is its own page, with a persistent contents sidebar and next/previous navigation between clauses — so the requirements can be read, linked to and cited directly, without a PDF reader.

Not an official publication. This is an unofficial accessible re-typesetting of the ETSI deliverable for reading convenience, and this version of the standard is a final draft under approval, not yet published. The ETSI PDF remains the authoritative version; see About this standard and the accessibility statement.
